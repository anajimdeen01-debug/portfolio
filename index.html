SANAD سند

AI underwriting and Shariah-compliance engine for corporate banking

Warba Bank Corporate Banking AI Challenge 2026, Track 1. Built with Olagunju Temitope.

Approving a corporate facility at an Islamic bank means reading dozens of pages of filings and checking each borrower’s income for Shariah compliance by hand. SANAD cross-checks a borrower’s documents, runs the Shariah screening, stress-tests debt-service coverage and drafts the bank’s credit documents. The live demo runs on a pre-loaded sample borrower dossier.

  • Flags conflicts between what a borrower declares and what its other filings show, citing the document, page and clause.
  • Deterministic Shariah screening based on AAOIFI Standards 21 and 35, including the purification amount owed to charity.
  • Interactive stress test of debt-service coverage against a 1.25x covenant floor, with a revenue-shock slider.
  • Generates four bank documents: a credit memorandum, a Murabaha term sheet, a relationship-manager brief and a Shariah board memo.

My role. Lead AI and financial-logic engineer: the Gemini reasoning pipelines, cross-document checking and the Shariah accounting engine. Olagunju Temitope built the backend, interface and deployment.

Built with React 19, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS, Python (FastAPI), Google Gemini 2.5, Docker, Netlify and Railway.

MediQuery AI

Natural-language interface for hospital databases

NACOS-UI-DATICAN Undergraduates Coding Competition for AI in Medicine, 2026. Team DataMed, built with Ganiyu Azeez Olayemi.

Doctors, nurses and hospital administrators often cannot get answers from their own patient records without SQL skills. In MediQuery AI they type a question in plain English, and an AI agent turns it into SQL, runs it against the hospital database and returns a readable table.

  • Four related tables (patients, diagnoses, prescriptions and lab results) filled with synthetic Nigerian patient data. No real patient records are used.
  • An agent tuned for a medical setting: it checks the database structure first and joins tables so results show names instead of raw IDs.
  • AES-256 encrypted database connections, session-based login, invite-code registration and rate limiting.

Built with React, Node.js and Express.js, n8n on Railway, OpenRouter (GPT-4o), MySQL on Aiven and Netlify. The code was submitted to the competition and confirmed received by the organizers.

DataChat AI

Chat with your data in plain English

Founder and developer of a B2B SaaS product.

DataChat AI lets businesses ask questions about their SQL databases and spreadsheets in plain English and get answers instantly. It has grown from a prototype into beta testing with early users.

  • The agent explores the database structure first, turns ID columns into readable names and asks for confirmation before destructive actions such as deleting data.
  • Excel support: analyse a spreadsheet without saving it, or import it into the database as a table to query later.
  • Built for several clients at once: AES-256 encrypted connections, session authentication, invite-code registration, rate limiting and per-user message limits.

Built with React, Node.js and Express.js, n8n on Railway, OpenRouter (GPT-4o), MySQL on Aiven and Netlify.
